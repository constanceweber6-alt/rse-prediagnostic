# CSR pre-assessment for IT and supply chain

A web app that assesses in about 10 minutes the CSR maturity of a subsidiary, a country or a supplier, and turns the answers into a prioritised action plan and a PDF report.

[Version française plus bas](#version-française)

## Why this project

Large international groups, in IT or with complex supply chains, face the same problems again and again:

- **Scope 3:** most of their emissions come from suppliers, cloud and transport, not from their own sites.
- **Due diligence:** the French Duty of Vigilance Law and the EU CSDDD require them to map and manage risks across their supply chain.
- **Consistency between countries:** a policy written at headquarters is not always applied in every subsidiary.
- **Supplier ratings:** strategic suppliers are asked for CSR ratings, and many of them do not know where to start.

I managed EcoVadis, CyberVadis and ISO 27001 certifications in a previous role. This tool turns that experience into something simple: a structured self-assessment that tells a team where it stands and what to do first.

## Features

- **20 questions on 4 themes** used by the main CSR ratings: environment and Green IT, labour and human rights, ethics and cybersecurity, sustainable procurement and due diligence.
- **Profile-aware:** the sector (IT, supply chain, hospitality, hardware) and the regions of operation change the weighting and the priorities.
- **Prioritised action plan:** each gap becomes a concrete action, ranked by impact, effort and context, with quick wins flagged.
- **Regulatory exposure:** CSRD, CSDDD, Duty of Vigilance, Sapin II, NIS 2 and GDPR are flagged when related practices are missing.
- **Help sheet for each action:** the evidence to gather for a CSR assessment and one KPI to track.
- **Radar chart, PDF report and CSV export** to share the results.
- **Improvement checklist:** 85 generic actions to tick and download.
- **Share link without a server:** answers are encoded in the page address, so a colleague opens the same assessment from a simple link.
- **French, English and Japanese** interface.

## How the scoring works

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Answer | 0 Nothing in place, 1 Started, 2 Formalised, 3 Deployed and measured in every country |
| Theme score | Sum of answers as a percentage of the maximum |
| Overall score | Themes weighted by sector (for example ethics and cybersecurity weigh more in IT) |
| Action priority | impact x (4 - effort) x gap, then adjusted for the relevant questions: x1.2 to x1.6 depending on sector, x1.2 if active in 3 regions or more, x1.3 if active in a higher-risk region |
| Level | Insufficient (under 25), Partial (25 to 44), Good (45 to 64), Advanced (65 and above) |

The scoring logic lives in `scoring.py` and is covered by unit tests.

> This is an indicative tool inspired by the structure of market CSR ratings. It does not replace an official assessment and is not affiliated with any rating platform.

## Two versions

| Version | Where | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Web (HTML and JavaScript) | `docs/index.html`, published with GitHub Pages | Instant demo in the browser, share link, export to PDF with the print button. No server, no data sent anywhere: answers stay in the browser |
| Python (Streamlit) | `app.py` | Full app with radar chart, generated PDF report and CSV export |

Both versions read the same questionnaire. After changing `questionnaire.py`, `translations_ja.py`, `help_sheets.py` or `tools/checklist_data.py`, run `python tools/export_json.py` to update the JSON files used by the web version.

## Run it locally

```bash
git clone https://github.com/constanceweber6-alt/rse-prediagnostic.git
cd rse-prediagnostic
pip install -r requirements.txt
streamlit run app.py
```

Run the tests:

```bash
pip install pytest
pytest
```

## Deploy it for free

1. Push the repository to GitHub.
2. Go to [share.streamlit.io](https://share.streamlit.io) and sign in with GitHub.
3. Choose the repository, the `main` branch and `app.py`, then click Deploy.

## Project structure

```
app.py            Streamlit interface
questionnaire.py  Questions, themes, sectors, regions and regulations (FR and EN)
translations_ja.py  Japanese texts for the app and the checklist
help_sheets.py    Evidence to gather and KPI for each action (FR, EN, JA)
scoring.py        Scores, levels, priorities and action plan
report.py         PDF report (ReportLab, Japanese font embedded)
i18n.py           Interface texts
packages.txt      Japanese font installed on Streamlit Cloud
tests/            Unit tests for the scoring logic
tools/            Export of the questionnaire for the web version
docs/             Web version (GitHub Pages) and example report
```

## Version française

# Pré-diagnostic RSE IT et supply chain

Une application web qui évalue en 10 minutes environ la maturité RSE d'une filiale, d'un pays ou d'un fournisseur, et transforme les réponses en plan d'actions priorisé et en rapport PDF.

### Pourquoi ce projet

Les grands groupes internationaux, dans l'IT ou avec des chaînes d'approvisionnement complexes, retrouvent toujours les mêmes difficultés : un scope 3 qui dépend surtout des fournisseurs, du cloud et du transport, des obligations de vigilance (loi française et directive européenne CSDDD), des politiques groupe pas toujours appliquées dans chaque filiale, et des fournisseurs qui ne savent pas par où commencer quand on leur demande une notation RSE.

J'ai piloté des certifications EcoVadis, CyberVadis et ISO 27001. Cet outil reprend cette expérience sous une forme simple : une auto-évaluation structurée qui montre où l'on en est et par quoi commencer.

### Fonctionnalités

- 20 questions sur 4 thèmes : environnement et Green IT, social et droits humains, éthique et cybersécurité, achats responsables et vigilance.
- Pondération et priorités adaptées au secteur et aux zones d'activité.
- Plan d'actions priorisé avec les actions rapides mises en avant.
- Réglementations à surveiller (CSRD, CSDDD, devoir de vigilance, Sapin II, NIS 2, RGPD).
- Graphique radar, rapport PDF et export CSV.
- Une fiche d'aide par action : preuves à rassembler et indicateur à suivre.
- Une page de 85 axes d'amélioration à cocher et à télécharger.
- Un lien de partage sans serveur : les réponses sont dans l'adresse de la page.
- Interface en français, en anglais et en japonais.

- **Bien démarrer :** https://constanceweber6-alt.github.io/rse-prediagnostic/guide.html (méthode en 5 étapes, les 7 questions centrales de l'ISO 26000 reliées à l'outil, guides gratuits)
